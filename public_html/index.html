Welcome to MagentaWeb

MagentaWeb is designed to be a lightweight platform on which a wide range of proprietary standards and apps can be used. At MagentaWeb, we place great importance on data protection (as long as you don't live in the US) and operate in the European Union in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation. If we/I ever start to set up or expand a server network, we will not have a single byte of data or any servers in the USA, due to the Cloud Act and other acts that require or enable the authorities of the United States of America (USA) to access our data in any form. Furthermore, if a US authority needs our data for any reason, it too (and especially it) must fill out a data request form in typical German fashion. Processing this form can take months or even years, given the speed of German authorities, but since we are in Germany and the operator of MagentaWeb does not like US data security and data protection, it should also be noted that, as with the IRS (the US tax authority, equivalent to the “Finanzamt” in Germany), the smallest error will result in immediate rejection.

Active projects

Core initiatives currently under development at MagentaWeb.

MagentaWeb System

The complete system for all of MagentaWeb, which includes MagentaHTML, MagentaSytle, MagentaScript, MagentaData, MagentaBackend and the MagentaNet Protocol.

MagentaNet Protocol

The MagentaNet Protocol is a protocol for all across MagentaWeb and MagentaNet, the MagentaNet Protocol uses magenta:// for unsecure connections and MAGENTA:// and magentasecure:// for secure connections.

MagentaHTML

A reimagined HTML layer: semantic, compact, and extendable for component-first workflows.

MagentaStyle

A modern alternative to CSS with simplified, composable rules and theme primitives.

MagentaScript

A lightweight scripting language with safe-by-default semantics and first-class async primitives.

MagentaData

A next-gen datastore abstraction focused on multi-device sync and privacy-centric replication.

MagentaBackend

Unified backend platform to replace common stacks (PHP, Node.js etc.) with a modular runtime.

FINDI

A smart route planner to plan your route and navigate you trough the local transport system on the local transport network of the Rhein-Sieg Transport Association (VRS).

How language switching works

The site stores your preference in the browser (localStorage) and updates the visible text instantly. Content is provided in English, German, French and Spanish.
