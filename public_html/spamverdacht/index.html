How to report a suspicious email?

Step 1 — Do not delete the email: Keep the suspicious email in your mailbox or forward it as an attachment (.eml) so full headers are maintained.
Step 2 — Forward: Forward the email to spamverdacht@magentaweb.org (German) or spamsuspect@magentaweb.org (international).
Step 3 — Subject & Info: Add "Spam Suspect" or "Spam Suspicion" in the subject and letter explain why the email seems suspicious (links, attachments, demands).
Step 4 — Provide metadata: If possible attach the email header or forward the message as an attachment. Note the received time (date & time) and the sender address.

What should be included in the report?

  • Email headers (as attachment or copied)
  • Sender address
  • Date & time of reception
  • Short reason why the email is suspicious
