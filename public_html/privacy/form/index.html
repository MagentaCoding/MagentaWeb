Information

Use these forms to submit privacy-related requests or report issues. The forms are divided into two categories:

  • MagentaWeb-related issues: Use this option if your privacy concern directly involves MagentaWeb, its services, or its platforms. This includes problems like data access requests, deletion requests, or reporting potential breaches within MagentaWeb.
  • Other services: Use this option if your privacy concern involves another company, service, or organization, but you prefer not to contact them directly. This allows MagentaHTML to assist in submitting the request on your behalf, ensuring your personal information is protected and your concerns are handled confidentially.

When using these forms, you may be asked to provide personal information such as your name, email address, and a description of the issue. This information is necessary to process your request effectively and to allow us to follow up with you if needed. All submitted information is stored securely and accessed only by authorized personnel.

By submitting a form, you maintain your rights under applicable data protection regulations. This includes the right to access your personal data, request corrections, object to processing, or request deletion. We handle all data according to strict privacy principles, ensuring confidentiality, integrity, and security throughout the process.

Please ensure that the information you provide is accurate and complete. Incomplete or incorrect information may delay the processing of your request. For any questions about these forms or how your data is managed, you may contact us directly at our official support channels.

Your trust is important to us. These forms are designed to simplify privacy requests, make them more accessible, and ensure that you have a safe and secure way to exercise your privacy rights without exposing yourself to unnecessary risks.
